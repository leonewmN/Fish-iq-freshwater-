What's different from saltwater?

Freshwater forecasts use wind, rainfall, temperature and daylight as planning indicators — not tides. Live river flow and water temperature aren't included in V1, so scores are indicative rather than catch probabilities.

⚠️ Safety & regulations

Always check Victorian Fisheries Authority rules for closed seasons, bag and size limits, trout waters, native fish, bait restrictions and local access. River levels can rise quickly; never assume a bank is safe or publicly accessible.
